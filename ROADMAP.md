# CC Vault Roadmap

## Sprint 1 — Foundation (complete)

- Application structure, configuration, and database schema
- Authentication, responsive dashboard, and starter card data
- Shared layout, navigation, and deployment documentation

## Sprint 2 — Cards and merchants

- Create, edit, archive, and filter cards
- Card benefits, fees, billing cycles, and renewal reminders
- Merchant directory with supported card offers

## Sprint 3 — Search and recommendations

- Global search across cards, benefits, and merchants
- Category and spend-based recommendations
- Comparison view for eligible cards

## Sprint 4 — Administration and reporting

- User management and role permissions
- Data import/export and audit trail
- Dashboard analytics and notification preferences

## Sprint 5 — Production polish

- Automated backups, monitoring, and accessibility review
- Performance tuning, security hardening, and launch checklist
